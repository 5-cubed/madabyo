# SEQ-017 Empty-state when the last tab in a pane closes
- Status: Draft

## Requirement
REQ-017

## Components
- TabManager (CMP-008)
- Pane (CMP-010)

## Flow
TabManager.closeTab(onlyRemainingTabId)
  |
  v
tabs.length === 0, activeTabId === null
  |
  v
<Pane /> (renders empty-state)

## Sequence
1. TabManager's `closeTab(fileId): void` is called on a pane with exactly one tab.
2. TabManager removes that entry, leaving `tabs.length === 0` and `activeTabId === null`.
3. Pane, receiving `tabs={[]}` and `activeTabId={null}` as props, renders its defined empty-state instead of a tab bar/content area.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. `tabs.length === 0` and `activeTabId === null` after closing a pane's only tab.
2. Pane renders the empty-state view (not a crash or blank area) for this prop combination.
