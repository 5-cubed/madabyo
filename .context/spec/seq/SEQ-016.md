# SEQ-016 Auto-activate adjacent tab after closing the active tab
- Status: Draft

## Requirement
REQ-016

## Components
- TabManager (CMP-008)

## Flow
TabManager.closeTab(activeTabId)
  |
  v
[tabs.length > 0?] --yes--> activeTabId = adjacent tab's id

## Sequence
1. TabManager's `closeTab(fileId): void` is called with `fileId === activeTabId`.
2. TabManager removes that entry from `tabs`.
3. If `tabs.length > 0` after removal, TabManager sets `activeTabId` to a deterministically chosen adjacent tab (the tab immediately to the left, or the next one to the right if none exists).

## Acceptance Criteria
1. After closing the active tab with at least one tab remaining, `activeTabId` is non-null and equals a tab still present in `tabs`.
2. The chosen adjacent tab follows the same rule every time (left-neighbor-first), verifiable across repeated closes.
